Nintendo Entertainment System (NES)

The NES helped revive video games after the 1983 market crash, redefining them as family entertainment. Released in 1985, this gray rectangular console was small, durable, and designed for easy use with interchangeable cartridges.

Although unassuming in appearance, its cultural impact was enormous. Games like Super Mario Bros. and The Legend of Zelda reimagined timeless mythic themes — quests, trials, and the hero’s journey — in digital form. In doing so, the NES introduced millions of players to interactive storytelling, transforming the living room into a stage for play and imagination.
